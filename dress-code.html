What to Wear

Dress Code

The theme is relaxed garden party attire.

Ladies
Midi or maxi length dresses. Light pastels, florals, and playful patterns are all warmly encouraged.
Gentlemen
Linen and light colours are encouraged. Ties aren't necessary, and blazers are entirely optional — keep it relaxed.
Please Note
White is reserved for the bride, and blue tones for the bridesmaids.
